# Replenish is a small vanilla+ mod themed around farming QOL in minecraft

## The current enchantments added are:

### Replenish (wow its the name of the mod)
Replenish can only be applied on hoes. Replenish's behavior depends on the tier of hoe its applied to.
- Below diamond quality hoes simply replant broken crops with seeds from either the loot of the crop broken, or from the player's inventory. However, this comes at the cost of half of the drops from the crop.
- Diamond and above quality hoes do the same thing but with full drops.
- Netherite and above quality hoes will fully prevent breaking non-mature crops.

### Green Foot
Green Foot can only be applied to boots (or other foot slot armor items). Green foot's behavior also depends on what tier of boots its applied to.
- Below diamond quality boots prevent trampling crops.
- Diamond and above quality boots do the same while also giving permanent speed 2 when worn. (Green Foot is balanced around having MoriyaShiine's enchancement installed with the single enchantment config option enabled, without that this enchantment is too OP in my opinion.)
